Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Timelix app handles personal data, in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD") and, where applicable, the laws of your region. By using the app, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Who is the controller

The data controller is [COMPANY NAME], registered under CNPJ [CNPJ], located at [ADDRESS], Brazil ("Meiratech"). You can reach our data protection officer at privacidade@meiratech.com.br.

2. Data that stays on your device

Timelix does not require sign-up or login. The timers you create, your settings and your usage history are stored only on your device and are not sent to Meiratech. If Android automatic backup is turned on, this data may be copied to your Google account under Google's own rules.

3. Data handled by third-party services

To show ads, measure usage and fix crashes, the app uses Google services that may collect device data:

ServiceDataPurpose
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, IP address, device model and system, ad interactionsShow ads and, if allowed, personalize them
Firebase AnalyticsApp usage events (for example, creating or starting a timer), installation ID, device model, language and approximate countryUnderstand how the app is used and improve it
Firebase CrashlyticsCrash reports, app state at the time of the crash and technical device dataFind and fix crashes
Google PlayData about the "Remove ads" purchase, handled by GoogleProcess and restore the purchase

Meiratech does not receive your payment details. Google's processing follows the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Legal bases

We rely on legitimate interest (LGPD, art. 7, IX) for personalized ads, analytics and crash reports, always with the opt-out described in section 5. Where local law requires consent, such as in the European Union, the app asks for your choice before using this data. Performance of a contract (art. 7, V) is the basis for processing the purchase.

5. Your choices in the app

  • Personalized ads: can be turned off in Settings > Privacy. Ads still appear, but without using your interests.
  • Share usage data: can be turned off in Settings > Privacy, which disables Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • Google privacy options: where the law of your region requires it, the app shows a consent message and a shortcut to change it.
  • Delete everything: in Settings you can delete all timers and the history from your device.
  • You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Sharing and international transfer

Meiratech does not sell personal data. The data described in section 3 is processed by Google, which may store it on servers outside Brazil, with the safeguards required by the LGPD (art. 33).

7. Retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data handled by Google services follows the retention periods set in those services and Google's policies.

8. Children and teenagers

Timelix is not directed to children under 13. Data of teenagers is handled in their best interest, as required by art. 14 of the LGPD.

9. Your rights

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing and review of consent, among other rights under art. 18 of the LGPD and the laws of your region. Because Meiratech does not keep your data on its own servers, many requests are handled by the options in the app or directly by Google. For any request, write to privacidade@meiratech.com.br. You may also file a complaint with your data protection authority.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as local storage on the device and services that encrypt data in transit.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version, and relevant changes will be announced in the app.
